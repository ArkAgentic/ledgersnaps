Effective date: 2026-10-05

1. What we collect

We collect account data (name, email, phone), authentication data (including OAuth identifiers), invoice metadata and extraction outputs, service usage logs, and security telemetry required to operate LedgerSnaps.

2. How we use data

We use data to authenticate users, process invoice extraction, create draft records in connected systems, provide support, prevent abuse, and improve service reliability.

3. Third-party services

We may process data through trusted providers required for service delivery, such as cloud infrastructure, payment processing, email, and connected accounting platforms (including Xero when you authorize it).

4. Data retention and security

We retain data only as needed for legal, operational, and security purposes. We apply access controls, encryption in transit, and secure secret handling for production systems.

5. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, or export of your personal data where applicable. You can also revoke connected app permissions in your provider account settings.

6. Contact

For privacy requests, contact: privacy@ledgersnaps.com
